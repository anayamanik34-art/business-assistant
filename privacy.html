Privacy Policy

Eksoch Business Assistant by Eksoch Analytics. Last updated: 24 November 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. Eksoch Analytics does not run a server for this app and never receives your data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

What stays on your device

Your business details, conversations, saved documents and your Anthropic API key are stored only inside this app on your device (in the browser's local storage).

What is sent to Anthropic

When you ask a question or use a tool, your message, the earlier messages in that conversation and your business details are sent directly from your device to Anthropic (api.anthropic.com) so the AI (Claude) can write the answer. This is billed to your own Anthropic account, and Anthropic's own privacy policy and terms apply to that data. Don't paste information you aren't allowed to share.

Your Pro code

If you enter a Pro code (for example from AppSumo), it is checked inside the app on your device and saved only there. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Removing your data

Open Settings → Delete all my data from this device, or uninstall the app.

Contact

Questions? Contact Eksoch Analytics through eksochanalytics.gumroad.com.
